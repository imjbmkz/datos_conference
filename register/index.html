Save your seat at DATOS

Free to attend. Registration runs through Meetup, and every RSVP is reviewed by the organizers.

Three steps

How registration works

  1. RSVP on Meetup

    Open the DATOS event on Meetup and answer the short registration questions.

  2. Wait for approval

    Seats are limited to 80 and every RSVP is reviewed by the organizers. You'll get a confirmation on Meetup once your spot is approved.

  3. Show up and say hi

    Bring your ID and arrive by 3:45 PM at Google Philippines Headquarters.

Reminders

  • Bring a valid government or school ID.
  • Have your approved Meetup RSVP ready at the registration desk.
  • Bring a laptop if you'd like to follow along; it's optional.
  • Dinner break runs from 5:35 PM to 6:20 PM. Dinner's on us!
