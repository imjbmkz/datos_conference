What to expect

An evening for everyone curious about data engineering

An evening of talks and a panel on cloud data engineering, AI, and building a data engineering career, organized by the Data Engineering Pilipinas community. Free to attend, open to everyone.

Cloud data engineering

Start from the fundamentals of building data pipelines on Google Cloud.

AI in real pipelines

Career panel

DEP community projects
