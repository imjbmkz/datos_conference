About DATOS

A conference by and for the Filipino data community

DATOS: The Data Engineering Pilipinas Conference is a community-run event from Data Engineering Pilipinas. 2026 is our pilot run.

The idea

Why DATOS

Datos is Filipino for data. DATOS brings people from our online community into one room to learn about the theories, practices and real-world applications of data engineering.

This year's theme is “Getting a Data Engineering Career in the Age of Artificial Intelligence.” As AI changes how data platforms are built, we want to help people understand what skills matter and how to get started or grow in the field.

The event features talks on cloud data engineering and AI, and a panel discussion that brings together different perspectives on how AI is shaping the field and the careers of data professionals.

Who should attend

  • Students exploring a career in data
  • Career shifters moving into data engineering
  • Data analysts, data scientists and software engineers
  • Data engineers and AI/ML engineers who want to compare notes
  • Hiring managers and recruiters building data teams

A welcoming space

Code of conduct

DATOS follows the Data Engineering Pilipinas code of conduct. We want everyone to feel safe and welcome, whatever their background or experience level.

Be kind, be respectful, and help each other learn. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated. If something makes you uncomfortable, talk to any organizer at the event.

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost?

DATOS is free. Seats are limited to 80, so registration goes through Meetup and every RSVP is reviewed.

How do I know if my registration is approved?

Meetup will notify you once an organizer approves your RSVP. If the event fills up, you'll be placed on the waitlist.

Who should attend?

Anyone curious about data engineering: students, career shifters, data analysts, data scientists, engineers and hiring managers. You don't need to be an expert.

Will the sessions be streamed or recorded?

We're planning to livestream the talks and panel. The link will be shared on this site and on the Data Engineering Pilipinas pages.

What should I bring?

A valid ID and your approved Meetup RSVP. A laptop is optional.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. DATOS follows the Data Engineering Pilipinas code of conduct. Everyone is expected to be kind, respectful and welcoming.
